# Hi, I'm Mayukh

I'm a cybersecurity researcher with an M.S. in Cybersecurity from Yeshiva University. My research interests lie at the intersection of **human-centered cybersecurity, social engineering, behavioral risk measurement, and simulation-based security research**.

I'm particularly interested in understanding how people make security decisions under pressure, what conventional awareness metrics fail to capture, and how we can study those behaviors more rigorously.

My current research explores these questions through VISHWAR and a growing body of literature reviews, methodological notes, and reproducible synthetic experiments.

### Research areas
- Human-centered and usable cybersecurity
- Social engineering and security decision-making
- Behavioral cyber-risk measurement
- Simulation-based research and evaluation
- AI and human–machine decision-making in security

### Selected research work

**[Human Cyber Risk Research](https://github.com/alienMak/human-cyber-risk-research)**

Public literature review, research questions, synthetic experiments, and methodological investigations.

**[VISHWAR](https://vishwar.com)**

Research-driven cybersecurity simulation platform studying human decision-making in social-engineering situations.

*Public experiments are exploratory and use synthetic data unless stated otherwise. They should not be interpreted as validated predictions of individual cyber risk.*

### Research interests

I'm interested in pursuing doctoral research exploring how behavioral evidence, simulation, and human factors can improve our understanding of cybersecurity decision-making.
